Biography & Perspective

The Intersection of Financial Rigor & Code

Born and based on Argentina's Atlantic coast in Necochea, my mission has always been driven by numbers, systems, and relentless optimization.

After graduating with a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) degree from the Facultad de Ciencias Económicas at Universidad Nacional de Mar del Plata (UNMdP), I immersed myself in audits, corporate taxes, financial statements, and business consulting.

Yet, working with modern companies revealed a stark bottleneck: manual spreadsheets and fragmented data silos were holding teams back. That spark led me into Data Science — graduating from Soy Henry's intensive bootcamp, mastering Python, SQL, GCP (Google Cloud Platform), machine learning pipelines, and executive dashboards in Power BI.

To close the loop and build fully autonomous digital products, I am currently completing the Full Stack Developer curriculum at Conquer Blocks (now at 40%). At 31, single and fully dedicated, I bring a unique dual-mindset as an international contractor: the prudence and precision of a licensed accountant paired with the architectural speed of a modern developer.
